# Reel Script Format

A vertical short-form video script (Instagram Reel / TikTok / YouTube Short),
roughly 30–45 seconds. Four beats, in this exact order. For each beat give the
ON-SCREEN TEXT (caption) and the SPOKEN line.

## The four beats (in order)

1. **Cold Frame** — open mid-action. No intro, no greeting, no "hey guys", no
   setup. A single visual or line that drops the viewer straight into the
   moment.
2. **Declaration** — one flat, definite statement of what this is or what was
   done. Stated as fact, not as hype.
3. **Proof** — the concrete evidence: what shipped, the number, the artifact,
   the before/after. Show it; do not claim it.
4. **Tomorrow hook** — one line pointing at what's next, leaving a reason to
   return. A forward pull, never "like and subscribe".

## Identity rules (non-negotiable)

- No catchphrases. No recurring bit. No slogans beyond the two specified below.
- No smiling at the camera. Delivery is flat, deadpan, work-first.
- The signature line **"Sirf kaam hai"** appears exactly once, on its own line,
  placed where it lands hardest (usually between Proof and the Tomorrow hook).
- The sign-off is exactly **"Day X. Done."** — substitute the day number for X
  if the topic implies one; otherwise close on the literal "Day X. Done."
- No exclamation marks. No emoji. No filler enthusiasm.

## Output shape

For each beat, output:

```
[Beat name]
ON-SCREEN: <caption>
SPOKEN: <line>
```

Then the signature line and the sign-off, each on its own line, as the close.
